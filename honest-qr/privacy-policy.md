# Privacy Policy — Honest QR for iPhone and iPad

Effective date: 2026-11-16

**In short:** Honest QR collects no data. No account, no servers of ours, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The
codes you scan and make stay on your device.

This policy explains how Honest QR ("the app", "we"), bundle ID `com.techdev.honestqr`, handles information.

## 1. What the app uses, and where it stays

| Information | Why | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| Camera picture | To read QR codes and barcodes live | Processed in memory on your device. Never recorded, saved or uploaded |
| Photos, screenshots and PDFs you pick or share to the app | To read the codes in them | Read once in memory on your device. The app does not keep a copy and has no access to your photo library |
| History (the text of codes you scan or make) | So you can find a code again | In the app's storage on your device. Wi-Fi passwords are removed before saving unless you turn on "Keep Wi-Fi passwords in History". You can switch History off, delete single entries or delete everything |
| My codes (codes you save, their style and an optional logo image you pick) | To show them full screen and on the widget | In the app's storage on your device |
| Settings | To remember your choices | On your device |

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, calendar, location, photos or any identifier. The link check
runs entirely on your device: the app never sends links to us or to any "safe browsing" service.

## 2. Permissions (all optional)

- **Camera**: to scan codes live. Without it you can still scan photos, screenshots and PDFs.
- **Add to Photos** (add-only): when you tap "Save to Photos" for a code you made. The app cannot see your library.
- **Face ID / Touch ID** (optional App lock): iOS checks it; the app only learns whether it succeeded.
- **Join Wi-Fi**: when you tap "Join network" on a scanned Wi-Fi code, the network name and password are handed to
  iOS, which asks you to confirm.
- **Contacts and Calendar**: the app does not read them. "Add to Contacts" and "Add to Calendar" open Apple's own
  screens; you decide whether to save.

## 3. When something leaves your device — only when you tap

- **Opening a link** from a code opens it in Safari (inside the app or the Safari app). The website then receives
  what any visited website receives.
- **Product lookup** ("Look up food", "Look up book", "Search the web") opens the public website of Open Food
  Facts, Open Library or DuckDuckGo with the barcode number in the address. The app itself makes no request.
- **Open in Maps / UPI app / Mail / Messages / Phone** hands the code's content to that app.
- **Share and export** (images, CSV, PDF, ZIP) go only where you send them.

## 4. Third-party services

None built in. No third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software. If you share analytics
with developers in iOS settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. Once a day the app asks the App Store
whether a newer version exists; the request contains only the app's ID and your store country.

## 5. Sharing, selling, children

We do not sell, rent or share any information. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including
children.

## 6. Keeping and deleting data

Everything stays on your device and in your own device backups. Delete single entries or everything in History
(History › More › Delete all), delete codes in My codes, or delete the app to remove all its data.

## 7. Honest limits

The link check looks only at a link's address with fixed rules (for example look-alike names, short links, bare
number addresses, unusual endings, app or profile downloads). It cannot see what a page contains and is not virus or
malware protection.

## 8. Changes

We update this page when the app changes; the effective date is at the top.
