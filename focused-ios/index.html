Privacy Policy

Focused: Screen Time Control for iPhone and iPad · Effective date: 27 October 2026

In short: Focused collects no data. Your Screen Time data is handled by Apple’s Screen Time framework and is only shown to you on your device. We have no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

This policy explains how Focused: Screen Time Control (“Focused”, “the app”, “we”), bundle ID com.techdev.focused, handles information on iPhone and iPad. The Android app has its own policy.

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, messages, browsing history or any identifier, and we cannot see which apps you use.

2.Permissions

  • Screen Time access (Apple’s Family Controls, for yourself): needed to show your screen time and to block the apps you choose. You approve it with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Screen Time or by deleting the app.
  • Notifications (optional): limit warnings, focus timer and schedule reminders. They are created on your device.
  • Face ID / Touch ID (optional App lock): iOS checks your face or fingerprint; the app only learns whether the check succeeded.

3.Exports, backups and sharing

  • You can export your own app data (focus history, programs, intentions) as a file using the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes. Apple’s Screen Time data can’t be exported.
  • Your app data may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of the device, which Apple and you control.
  • Share buttons in the app share only a link to the app or text such as “Day 7 of 21 done”, never your usage data.

4.Third-party services

None. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software. If you choose to share crash reports with developers in iOS settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports.

5.Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share any information. We never receive your usage data.

6.Children

Focused is a self-control app for the person using the device. It is not a parental-control app. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

7.Keeping and deleting data

Everything stays on your device. Delete the app to remove all its data. Turning off Screen Time access in Settings stops the app from reading Screen Time data and removes its blocks.

8.Security

Your data stays in the app’s private storage protected by iOS, and the app does not send it over the internet.

9.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The current version and its effective date are always on this page and in the app (Me → Privacy policy).
