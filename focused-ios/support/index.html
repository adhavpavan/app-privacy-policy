Focused: Screen Time Control for iPhone and iPad

Need help? Write to tecdroiddeveloper@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were trying to do.

1.Getting started

  • Open Focused and tap Continue. Apple asks you to allow Screen Time access with Face ID or your passcode. Focused uses it only on your own iPhone.
  • Choose the apps you want to manage, then start a focus session or add a limit.
  • Your screen time is shown on the Today and Stats tabs. It never leaves your iPhone.

2.You are always in control

  • End a focus session: open Focused and tap End focus, or use End on the Lock Screen.
  • Pause or turn off a limit: Limits tab › the limit › turn it off or delete it.
  • Turn off Screen Time access: Settings › Screen Time, or delete the app. All blocks are removed.

3.Safari Lite mode

Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Focused Lite › Allow Extension, then allow it on instagram.com, youtube.com, facebook.com and x.com. It works only on those websites in Safari, not in the apps. Focused is not affiliated with Meta, Google or X.

4.Focused Pro, purchases and subscriptions

  • Blocking, limits and stats are free. Focused Pro adds extra features.
  • Restore a purchase: Me › Focused Pro › Restore purchases.
  • Cancel a subscription: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Focused, at least 24 hours before it renews.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
