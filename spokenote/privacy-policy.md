# Privacy Policy — Spokenote for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Effective date: 2026-11-16

**In short:** Spokenote collects no data. No account, no servers, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your recordings,
transcripts and summaries are made and kept on your device. Your voice is never uploaded by the app.

This policy explains how Spokenote ("the app", "we"), bundle ID `com.techdev.spokenote`, handles information.

## 1. What the app uses, and where it stays
| Information | Why | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| Audio recordings and audio files you import | The notes you make | On your device, in the app's private storage |
| Transcripts | Made on your device by Apple's speech recognition (SpeechAnalyzer) | On your device |
| Summaries, key points, action items, titles | Made on your device by Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence, Foundation Models) | On your device |
| Folders, tags, favourites, bookmarks, settings | To organise your notes | On your device |
| Titles, dates and lengths of your 3 latest notes | Shown in Spokenote's widgets | On your device (shared only with Spokenote's own widgets) |
| Purchases (if offered in a later version) | To unlock Pro features | Handled by Apple. We receive no payment details |

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, location, photos or any identifier. We cannot see your
recordings, transcripts or summaries.

**Speech recognition:** transcription runs on your device. The first time you transcribe in a language, your device may
download that language's speech model from Apple; your audio is not sent. Spokenote does not use Apple's server-based
speech recognition.

**AI summaries:** made by Apple's on-device model only. Spokenote does not send your text to any cloud AI service. On
devices without Apple Intelligence, summaries are simply not offered.

## 2. Permissions
- **Microphone:** to record when you tap Record. The app never records on its own. If you refuse, you can still import,
  play and apply effects to audio.
- **Speech recognition** (only on devices that use Apple's dictation engine for your language): to transcribe on your
  device.
- **Face ID / Touch ID** (optional App lock): your device checks it; the app only learns whether it succeeded.
- **Files you pick** (import): the app copies only the files you choose.

## 3. Third-party services
None. No third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software. If you share analytics with
developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. Once a day the app asks the App Store
whether a newer version exists; the request contains only the app's ID and your store country.

## 4. Sharing, selling, children
We do not sell, rent or share any information. When you use Share, the file goes only where you send it. The app
collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## 5. Recording other people
Recording people may require their consent where you live. You are responsible for following those laws. Spokenote has
no hidden or silent recording mode; your device shows its microphone indicator while recording.

## 6. Keeping and deleting data
Everything stays on your device and in your own device backups (for example iCloud Backup, if you use it). Deleting a
note deletes its audio, transcript and summary. Settings › Delete all notes removes every note. Deleting the app
removes all its data.

## 7. Changes
We update this page when the app changes; the effective date is at the top.
