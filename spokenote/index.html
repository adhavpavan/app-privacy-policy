Privacy Policy

Spokenote for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch · Effective 2026-11-17

In short: Spokenote collects no data. No account, no servers, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your recordings, transcripts and summaries are made and kept on your device. Your voice is never uploaded by the app.

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, location, photos or any identifier. We cannot see your recordings, transcripts or summaries.

Speech recognition runs on your device. The first time you transcribe in a language, your device may download that language's speech model from Apple; your audio is not sent. Spokenote does not use Apple's server-based speech recognition.

AI summaries are made by Apple's on-device model only. Spokenote does not send your text to any cloud AI service. On devices without Apple Intelligence, summaries are simply not offered.

2.Permissions

  • Microphone: to record when you tap Record (on iPhone, iPad, Mac or Apple Watch). The app never records on its own. If you refuse, you can still import, play and apply effects to audio.
  • Speech recognition (only on devices that use Apple's dictation engine for your language): to transcribe on your device.
  • Reminders (optional, only when you tap “Add to Reminders”): to add the tasks you pick to your Reminders list. Spokenote only adds items; it does not read or upload your reminders.
  • Face ID / Touch ID (optional App lock): your device checks it; the app only learns whether it succeeded.
  • Files you pick (import): the app copies only the files you choose.

3.Third-party services

None. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software. If you share analytics with developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. The app asks the App Store once a day whether a newer version exists; that request contains only the app's ID and your store country.

4.Sharing, selling, children

We do not sell, rent or share any information. When you use Share, the file goes only where you send it. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

5.Recording other people

Recording people may require their consent where you live. You are responsible for following those laws. Spokenote has no hidden or silent recording mode; your device shows its microphone indicator while recording.

6.Keeping and deleting data

Everything stays on your device and in your own device backups (for example iCloud Backup, if you use it). Deleting a note deletes its audio, transcript and summary. Settings › Delete all notes removes every note. Deleting the app removes all its data.
