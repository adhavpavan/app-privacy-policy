Support

Spokenote for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch

Need help? Write to tecdroiddeveloper@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days. Please include your device model, iOS version and what you were trying to do (Settings › Contact support fills in the app version for you).

1.Getting started

  • Record: open the Record tab and tap the big button. Tap it again to stop and save. Recording keeps going with the screen locked.
  • Transcript: new recordings are transcribed on your device automatically (Settings › Transcribe new recordings). Tap any word to jump there.
  • Summary: on devices with Apple Intelligence, a summary, key points and action items appear after the transcript.
  • Apple Watch: install Spokenote on your Watch (Watch app on iPhone › Available Apps), tap the big button to record, tap Stop to save. The recording goes to Spokenote on your iPhone (even later, if your iPhone is away), which transcribes, summarises and names it like any other note. Add the Spokenote complication or Smart Stack widget to start recording in one tap.
  • Voice effects: open Studio, pick a note and tap an effect to hear it; save it as a new note or share it.

2.Common questions

  • No summary? Summaries need Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later, iPad or Mac with M1 or later), turned on in Settings, in a supported language. Transcripts work on every device.
  • "Transcription isn't available for this language": pick another language in Settings › Language under Transcripts. The first transcription in a language downloads Apple's speech model once.
  • Start from anywhere: add the Record control to Control Center or the Action button, or say "Start recording in Spokenote". iOS requires the app to open to start recording.
  • Watch recording not on my iPhone yet? Keep the Watch near your iPhone and open Spokenote on both. The Watch's Recent page shows "Waiting to send", "Sending to iPhone" or "On iPhone"; you can play a recording on the Watch until it arrives. If the Watch microphone is off: Settings on the Watch › Privacy & Security › Microphone.
  • Import: Record or Notes tab › Import audio (audio and video files from Files).
  • Phone calls: iOS does not let apps record calls.
  • Change the look: Settings › Appearance (System, Light, Dark and 10 accent colours).
  • Hide tabs: Settings › Customize tabs.
  • App lock: Settings › App lock. Your device passcode always works as a fallback.
  • Language: Settings › Language opens the Settings app, where you pick the app's language.

3.Purchases (only when Pro is sold)

  • Restore a purchase: Settings › Restore purchases.
  • Cancel a subscription: Settings › your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before it renews.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
