Privacy Policy

Image Converter for Android · Effective date: 1 November 2026

In short: Image Converter converts the photos and PDFs you pick, on your phone. There is no account and no server of ours. No photo, PDF or file name is ever uploaded. The app asks for no storage, photo or camera permission. To fix crashes and understand general usage, release builds use Google Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics. Those reports never include your photos, PDFs or file names. We never sell data.

1.What the app processes, and where

All processing happens on your device. History and settings are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. The image and PDF files themselves are not part of the app's backup.

The app does not read your location, contacts, messages, accounts or the list of your apps. It does not browse your photo library: you pick each file.

2.Permissions

  • No storage, photo or camera permission. Input comes from the Android photo picker, the file picker, or a share into the app. Camera to PDF opens Android's own camera into a file the app provides; the camera permission is not declared.
  • Internet and network state — Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Analytics and Google Play only. Converting works fully offline.
  • Advertising ID — used by Firebase Analytics in release builds.

3.Exports, backups and sharing

Results are saved to Pictures/Image Converter and Documents/Image Converter, or to a folder you choose. When you share a result, Android's share sheet sends it only to the app you pick, under that app's own policy. Android may include history, recipes and settings in your own device backup, which Android and Google manage and encrypt. The image and PDF files themselves are not part of the app's backup. When you use Contact us, your e-mail app opens with the app version, Android version and phone model added so we can help; you decide whether to send it.

4.Third-party services

Release builds use Google services that follow Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy):

  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports with device model, Android version, app version and app state, and a Crashlytics installation ID. https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Firebase Analytics — basic, anonymous usage statistics such as app opens and which screens are used, with an app instance ID and the advertising ID. Photos, PDFs and file names are not sent.
  • Google Play in-app updates and in-app review — to offer updates and, occasionally, a review prompt.
  • Google Play Billing — for a Pro purchase. Google handles the payment; the app only learns whether Pro is owned by your Google account.
  • Google AdMob — included for possible future ads, but turned off: no ads are shown and AdMob is never started. If ads are ever turned on, this policy will be updated first and the app will ask for consent where the law requires it.

5.Purchases

Pro is bought through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment. The app only learns whether Pro is owned by your Google account.

6.Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. Google processes the Firebase and Play data above as our service provider.

7.Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8.Keeping and deleting data

History stays on your phone for 30 days by default. You can keep it forever, turn it off or clear it in Settings. Clearing the history never deletes your files. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes the history, recipes and settings. Files already saved to Pictures or Documents stay where you put them; delete those yourself. Firebase keeps crash reports for about 90 days and analytics data according to its retention settings. To ask for deletion of crash or analytics data, write to tecdroiddeveloper@gmail.com with the app instance ID shown in the app's About screen. Deletion page: https://adhavpavan.github.io/app-privacy-policy/image-converter/delete-data/

9.Security

App data is kept in Android's private app storage, which other apps cannot read. Connections to Google services use encryption (HTTPS). No photo, PDF or file name is sent over the internet by the app.

10.Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The current version, with its effective date, is always in the app (menu › Privacy policy) and at https://adhavpavan.github.io/app-privacy-policy/image-converter/.

11.Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: tecdroiddeveloper@gmail.com. When you use Contact us, your e-mail app adds the app version, Android version and phone model so we can help.
