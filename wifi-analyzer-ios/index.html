Privacy Policy

WiFi Analyzer for iPhone, iPad and Mac · Effective 2026-11-21

In short: WiFi Analyzer collects no data. No account, no servers of ours, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your device list, labels and scan history stay on your device (and, only if you turn on iCloud sync (when available), in your own private iCloud).

2.Network traffic

  • Your local network: scanning and tools (ping, traceroute, port scan, Wake-on-LAN, speed between your devices) talk only to devices on the network you are on, or to the address you type.
  • Internet speed test (optional): runs only when you tap Start. It downloads and uploads test data with Apple's network quality test server (mensura.cdn-apple.com). We add no identifiers.
  • DNS lookup: uses your device's normal DNS (usually your router or provider).
  • Whois (optional): runs only when you look something up. The domain or address you type is sent to whois.iana.org and to the registry server it refers to (port 43). Nothing else is sent.
  • iCloud sync (optional, off by default, when available): if you turn it on in Settings, your device labels are stored in your own private iCloud database by Apple's CloudKit. Only your Apple Account can read it; we have no access and no server of our own. Turning it off stops syncing; you can delete the iCloud copy in your Apple Account's iCloud storage settings.

3.Permissions

  • Local Network: to find devices on your Wi-Fi and run the tools you choose.
  • Location (optional, while using the app): iOS and macOS share the Wi-Fi name only with apps that have location access. The app uses it only to show the network name. Your location is never stored, sent or used in the background.
  • Notifications (optional): new-device alerts, created on your device.
  • Face ID / Touch ID / password (optional App lock): iOS or macOS checks it; the app only learns whether it succeeded.

4.Third-party services

No third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software. If you share analytics with developers in iOS settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. Once a day the app asks the App Store whether a newer version exists; the request contains only the app's ID and your store country. On-device explanations (where available) run on Apple Intelligence on your device. Optional iCloud sync (when available) uses Apple's iCloud under your own Apple Account; Whois servers receive only the name you look up.

5.Sharing, selling, children

We do not sell, rent or share any information. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

6.Keeping and deleting data

Everything stays on your device and in your own device backups (and your private iCloud if you turn on iCloud sync (when available)). "Forget this device" removes one device; deleting the app removes all its data. Exports (CSV/JSON) go only where you choose to share them.
