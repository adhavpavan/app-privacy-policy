Privacy Policy

QR Code Scanner & Generator for Android · Effective date: 8 November 2026

In short: what you scan and create stays on your phone. There is no account and no server of ours. Camera frames are read in memory to find codes and are never saved or sent. Reading a code and checking a link work fully offline: the app never looks up, fetches or follows a scanned link by itself. To fix crashes and understand general usage, release builds use Google Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) and Firebase Analytics (basic app events such as app opens and screens viewed, with device identifiers including the advertising ID). These never include your scans, history or codes. This version shows no ads. We never sell data.

2.Permissions and how they are used

Camera (CAMERA)

To scan codes. Asked on the welcome screen when you tap Allow, or when you first open the Scan tab. Frames are read in memory only. The camera is optional: you can scan from pictures instead, and turn the permission off any time in Android settings.

Wi-Fi (ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_STATE)

Only when you tap "Join" on a scanned Wi-Fi code, to hand that network to Android. Depending on the Android version, Android shows its own "Save network?" sheet or a suggestion notification and you decide. The app does not read or list your saved networks.

Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)

Used only by Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Analytics and Google Play services (updates, review, purchases). Scanning, the link checker and code creation never use the network. Opening a link or searching the web happens only when you tap that action, and then your browser or the website handles it under its own policy.

Advertising ID (AD_ID)

Read by Firebase Analytics (release builds). No ads are shown in this version.

Fingerprint, face or screen lock (USE_BIOMETRIC, USE_FINGERPRINT)

Only if you turn on Wallet › Cards › Lock cards. Android shows its own fingerprint, face or screen-lock prompt and tells the app only whether it worked. The app never sees, stores or sends biometric data. Added by Android's biometric library; nothing is asked until you turn the lock on.

Vibration (VIBRATE)

Short haptic feedback when a code is read or a button is pressed. You can turn haptics off in Settings.

3.Exports, backups, restore, widget and feedback

  • Exports and sharing. When you share a result, export history (CSV or JSON), make a full backup or save a code as an image, PDF or SVG, the file is created on your phone and saved only where you choose with Android's file picker, or sent with the app you pick in the share sheet. If the file would include Wi-Fi codes, the app asks first whether it may contain their passwords.
  • Restore. Restoring a backup reads only the file you pick and adds its contents to the app. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Widgets. The Scan widget opens the scanner. The My code and My card widgets show a code you chose on your home screen, so anyone who can see your home screen can see and scan it. Widget data stays on your phone; remove the widget to hide it.
  • Android backup. Android may include the app's database (history and My codes), settings and logo pictures in your own device backup (for example your Google account backup) or a phone-to-phone transfer, if you turned that on. Android and Google manage and encrypt it; we cannot read it.
  • Feedback. "Contact us" opens your e-mail app with the app version, Android version and phone model added so we can help. You decide whether to send it.

4.Third-party services

Release builds include Google components that follow Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy):

  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and error reports: stack traces, device model, Android version, app version, app state at the time, and a Crashlytics installation ID. https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Firebase Analytics — automatically collected events (first open, sessions, screens viewed) with an app instance ID and the advertising ID. The content of your scans, history and codes is never sent.
  • Google ML Kit barcode scanning — reads codes on your phone with a model bundled in the app.
  • Google Play in-app updates and in-app review — to offer updates and, now and then, a review prompt.
  • Google Play Billing — if Pro is offered, Google handles the payment; the app only learns whether Pro is owned by your Google account.
  • Google AdMob — included for possible future ads but turned off: no ads are shown and AdMob is never started. If ads are ever turned on, this policy will be updated first and the app will ask for consent where the law requires it.

5.Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. Google processes the Firebase and Play data above as our service provider.

6.Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7.Keeping and deleting your data

History and My codes stay on your phone until you delete them. You can delete single scans, clear all history in Settings, or set history to auto-delete after 30 or 90 days. Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › QR Code Scanner & Generator › Storage › Clear storage, removes everything on the phone. Files you exported are not touched; delete them where you saved them. Firebase keeps crash reports for about 90 days and analytics data according to its retention settings. To ask for deletion of crash or analytics data, write to tecdroiddeveloper@gmail.com with the app instance ID shown in the app's About screen. Deletion page: https://adhavpavan.github.io/app-privacy-policy/qr-scanner/delete-data/

8.Security

App data is kept in Android's private app storage, which other apps cannot read. Connections to Google services are encrypted (HTTPS). No camera image, scan or code is sent anywhere by the app.
