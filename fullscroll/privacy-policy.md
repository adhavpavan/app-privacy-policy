# Privacy Policy — Fullscroll for iPhone and iPad

Effective date: 2026-11-27

**In short:** Fullscroll collects no data. No account, no servers, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. What you create
stays on your device.

This policy explains how Fullscroll ("the app", "we"), bundle ID `com.techdev.fullscroll`, handles information.

## 1. What the app uses, and where it stays
| Information | Why | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| Screenshots, photos, screen recordings and PDFs you choose | To join them into one long image | Copied into the app's private storage on your device |
| Your projects (stitches, edits, hidden areas, markup) and settings | So you can edit and export again | On your device (the app's private storage) |
| Text and private details found in your images (on-device text recognition, face and code detection) | Copy text, searchable PDFs, "Hide private info" | On your device. Never uploaded |
| Web pages you ask it to capture | To make a long image of that page | Loaded directly from that website; the result stays on your device |
| Purchases (if offered) | To unlock Pro features | Handled by Apple. We receive no payment details |

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, location, photos, screen recordings or any identifier. Nothing
you stitch, record or capture is sent to us or to anyone else.

## 2. Permissions
- **Photos** (optional): picking images uses the system picker and needs no permission. Full or limited access is
  asked only if you turn on screenshot suggestions or use the "Stitch Last Screenshots" shortcut. "Add only" access is
  asked the first time you save a result to Photos.
- **Screen recording** (Scroll Capture, optional): iOS asks you to start it every time and shows a red indicator while
  it runs. The recording is saved on your device for stitching and deleted afterwards. No sound is recorded.
- **Notifications** (optional): "Your Scroll Capture is ready", created on your device.
- **Face ID / Touch ID** (optional App lock): iOS checks it; the app only learns whether it succeeded.

## 3. Third-party services
None. No third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software. Web capture loads only the address
you type or share, in a private browser view without cookies from Safari. If you share analytics with developers in
iOS settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. Once a day the app asks the App Store whether a newer version
exists; the request contains only the app's ID and your store country.

## 4. Sharing, selling, children
We do not sell, rent or share any information. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including
children.

## 5. Keeping and deleting data
Everything stays on your device and in your own device backups. Delete the app to remove all its data.

## 6. Changes
We update this page when the app changes; the effective date is at the top.
