Privacy Policy

Fullscroll for iPhone and iPad · Effective 2026-11-27

In short: Fullscroll collects no data. No account, no servers, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. What you create stays on your device.

1.What the app uses, and where it stays

InformationWhyWhere it stays
Screenshots, photos, screen recordings and PDFs you chooseTo join them into one long imageCopied into the app's private storage on your device
Your projects (stitches, edits, hidden areas, markup) and settingsSo you can edit and export againOn your device (the app's private storage)

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, location, photos, screen recordings or any identifier. Nothing you stitch, record or capture is sent to us or to anyone else.

2.Permissions

  • Photos (optional): picking images uses the system picker and needs no permission. Access is asked only for screenshot suggestions or the "Stitch Last Screenshots" shortcut; "add only" access the first time you save to Photos.
  • Screen recording (Scroll Capture, optional): iOS asks you to start it every time and shows a red indicator while it runs. The recording stays on your device and is deleted after stitching. No sound.
  • Notifications (optional): "Your Scroll Capture is ready", created on your device.
  • Face ID / Touch ID (optional App lock): iOS checks it; the app only learns whether it succeeded.

3.Third-party services

None. Web capture loads only the address you type or share, in a private browser view. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software. If you share analytics with developers in iOS settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. The app asks the App Store once a day whether a newer version exists; that request contains only the app's ID and your store country.

4.Sharing, selling, children

We do not sell, rent or share any information. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

5.Keeping and deleting data

Everything stays on your device and in your own device backups. Delete the app to remove all its data.

6.Changes and contact

We update this page when the app changes; the effective date is at the top. Questions: tecdroiddeveloper@gmail.com · Support
