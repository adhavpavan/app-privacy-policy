# Privacy Policy: WidgetWell for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Vision Pro and Apple Watch

Effective date: 2026-11-28

**In short:** WidgetWell collects no data. No account, no servers of ours, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. What you
create stays on your devices.

This policy explains how WidgetWell ("the app", "we"), bundle ID `com.techdev.widgetwell`, handles information.

## 1. What the app uses, and where it stays
| Information | Why | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| Your designs, lists, habits, countdowns, notes and settings | To draw your widgets | On your device; in your own iCloud only if you turn on iCloud sync |
| Photos you pick | To show them in photo widgets | Small copies on your device, never uploaded |
| Location (approximate) | Local weather and sunrise times | Used on the device; rounded coordinates go to the weather service below |
| Calendar events and reminders | Calendar and to-do widgets | On your device |
| Health (steps, activity, sleep, read only) | Health widgets | On your device; never shared |
| Purchases (if offered later) | To unlock Pro | Handled by Apple; we receive no payment details |

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, precise location, photos or any identifier.

## 2. Services the app contacts
| Service | When | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| Apple Weather (WeatherKit) | You use a weather widget | Approximate location (Apple's privacy policy applies) |
| Open-Meteo | Only if Apple Weather is unavailable, or for air quality | Coordinates rounded to about 1 km |
| App Store | Once a day, to check for a newer version | The app's ID and your store country |

None of these requests contains an identifier, account or anything linked to you.

## 3. Permissions
Each permission is asked only when you use a feature that needs it, and the app works without it: Location, Calendars,
Reminders, Photos, Health, Notifications, Face ID (App lock). You can change them any time in Settings.

## 4. Sharing, selling, children
We do not sell, rent or share any information. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including
children.

## 5. Keeping and deleting data
Everything stays on your devices and in your own backups or iCloud. Delete the app (and turn off iCloud sync) to remove
all its data.

## 6. Changes
We update this page when the app changes; the effective date is at the top.
