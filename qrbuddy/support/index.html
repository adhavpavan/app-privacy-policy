Support

ScanIt for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch

Need help? Write to tecdroiddeveloper@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days. Please include your device model, iOS version and what you were trying to do (Settings › Contact support fills in the app version for you).

1.Getting started

  • Scan: tap Scan on Home and point at a code. A small card shows what's inside before anything opens; tap › for the full result.
  • Photos, screenshots, PDFs: on Home tap Photo, PDF or Paste, drop a file on Home (iPad), or in another app tap Share › "Scan with ScanIt".
  • Open straight into the camera: Settings › Scanning › Open on launch › Camera.
  • The camera looks zoomed in: Settings › Scanning › Camera view › Fit (like the Camera app), and Default zoom (0.5×–4× or Auto).
  • Several codes in view: each gets a number. Tap the one you want, or tap Add all. Multi-scan counts many items and shows the count on your Lock Screen.
  • Make a code: Create tab › pick a type › fill in › style it (colours, shapes, logo, caption) › Save. Save is available once the code has been read back on your device. Your codes sit at the top of Create.
  • Backup: Settings › Data & backup › Back up to a file. Restore adds what's missing and never deletes.

2.Common questions

  • What does the link check do? It reads the link's address on your device and looks for warning signs: look-alike names of well-known sites, special characters that imitate letters, short links, bare number addresses, unusual endings, app or profile downloads, http without encryption. "No warning signs" means none matched; it cannot see the page itself and is not virus protection. Safari's own warnings still apply.
  • Where links open: Settings › Links › Open in (ScanIt, Safari or Ask), and "Confirm every link".
  • The camera is black or asks for permission: Settings app › ScanIt › Camera. Photo and PDF scanning work without it.
  • "Join" fails for Wi-Fi: check the password in the code; iOS shows its own confirmation. Some networks (enterprise / captive portal) can't be joined from a code.
  • My code says "Doesn't scan": use more contrast (dark code on light background), a larger quiet zone, square dots, or remove the logo.
  • Deleted something by mistake: tap Undo, or History › More › Recently Deleted (kept 30 days).
  • Tags: add tags from a result (More › Tags) or when saving a code; filter History and Your codes by tag.
  • iCloud sync: Settings › iCloud sync. Keeps History and Your codes the same on your iPhone and iPad, through your own iCloud Drive. Needs iCloud Drive turned on for ScanIt in the Settings app.
  • Apple Watch: install ScanIt on your Watch from the Watch app on iPhone. Your codes appear there and work without your iPhone nearby. Add the "Favourite code" complication to a watch face.
  • Wi-Fi passwords in History: removed by default. Settings › Scanning › Keep Wi-Fi passwords to keep them.
  • Export History: History › More › Export CSV, Excel CSV or JSON.
  • Widgets: Home Screen or Lock Screen › Edit › Add Widget › ScanIt: Scan, Recent scans, or My code (touch and hold the widget › Edit Widget to choose the code).
  • Control Center and Action button: add ScanIt's "Scan code", "Scan a photo" or "Show my Wi-Fi code" controls.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: "Scan with ScanIt", "Show my Wi-Fi code in ScanIt", "Check a link with ScanIt".
  • UPI codes: ScanIt makes a static code with your own UPI ID. It is not affiliated with NPCI or any bank and never handles money; check the code with your UPI app before sharing it.
  • Look and language: Settings › Personalize › Appearance (light, dark, colours, app icon); Language opens the Settings app.
  • App lock: Settings › Privacy & access › App lock. Your device passcode always works as a fallback.
  • ScanIt closed unexpectedly: it opens in safe mode after repeated crashes; tap "Tell us what happened" on Home to e-mail us.
