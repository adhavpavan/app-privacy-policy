# Privacy Policy — ScanIt for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch

Effective date: 2026-11-16 (updated 2026-11-23: new name ScanIt, optional iCloud sync, Apple Watch, Recently Deleted,
tags, widgets, Siri and Spotlight)

**In short:** ScanIt (formerly QR Buddy) collects no data. No account, no servers of ours, no ads, no analytics, no
tracking. The codes you scan and make stay on your devices, and in your own iCloud only if you turn on iCloud sync.

This policy explains how ScanIt ("the app", "we"), bundle ID `com.techdev.qrbuddy`, handles information.

## 1. What the app uses, and where it stays

| Information | Why | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| Camera picture | To read QR codes and barcodes live | Processed in memory on your device. Never recorded, saved or uploaded |
| Photos, screenshots and PDFs you pick or share to the app | To read the codes in them | Read once in memory on your device. The app does not keep a copy and has no access to your photo library |
| History (the text of codes you scan or make, with your notes and tags) | So you can find a code again | On your device. Wi-Fi passwords are removed before saving unless you turn on "Keep Wi-Fi passwords in History". You can switch History off or delete entries; deleted entries wait 30 days in Recently Deleted (so you can undo) and are then removed for good |
| Your codes (codes you save, their style, tags and an optional logo image you pick) | To show them full screen, on widgets and on your Apple Watch | On your device; copied straight to your paired Apple Watch if you install the Watch app |
| CSV / text files you import for Bulk create | To make many codes at once | Read once in memory on your device; not kept |
| Backup files you make or restore (Settings › Data & backup) | To keep a copy of History and Your codes, or bring them back | Made on your device and saved only where you choose (for example Files or iCloud Drive). ScanIt uploads nothing |
| iCloud sync (off by default; Settings › iCloud sync) | To keep History and Your codes the same on your iPhone and iPad | In **your own** iCloud Drive, in the app's private folder under your Apple Account. We cannot see it; Apple stores it under its own privacy policy. Turning sync off keeps everything on the device |
| Clipboard | Paste reads a copied image or link only when you tap Paste; "Copy automatically" (off by default) copies what you scan | On your device |
| Settings | To remember your choices | On your device |

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, calendar, location, photos or any identifier. The link check
runs entirely on your device: the app never sends links to us or to any "safe browsing" service.

## 2. Permissions (all optional)

- **Camera**: to scan codes live. Without it you can still scan photos, screenshots and PDFs.
- **Add to Photos** (add-only): when you tap "Save to Photos" for a code you made. The app cannot see your library.
- **Face ID / Touch ID** (optional App lock): iOS checks it; the app only learns whether it succeeded.
- **Join Wi-Fi**: when you tap "Join" on a scanned Wi-Fi code, the network name and password are handed to iOS, which
  asks you to confirm.
- **Contacts and Calendar**: the app does not read them. "Add to Contacts" and "Add to Calendar" open Apple's own
  screens; you decide whether to save.
- **Live Activities**: during Multi-scan, the count and the short title of the last code appear on your Lock Screen.

## 3. When something leaves your device — only when you choose

- **Opening a link** from a code opens it in Safari (inside the app or the Safari app, as you choose in Settings). The
  website then receives what any visited website receives.
- **Product lookup** ("Look up product", "Look up book", "Search the web") opens the public website of Open Food
  Facts, Open Library or DuckDuckGo with the barcode number in the address. The app itself makes no request.
- **Open in Maps / UPI app / Mail / Messages / Phone** hands the code's content to that app.
- **Share and export** (images, SVG, CSV, JSON, PDF, ZIP, backup files) go only where you send them.
- **Print** sends the page to the printer you pick in the iOS print sheet.
- **iCloud sync**, if you turn it on, copies History and Your codes to your own iCloud Drive (see section 1).
- **Apple Watch**: Your codes go directly from your iPhone to your paired Watch, never through us.
- **Widgets, Siri, Shortcuts and Spotlight** show your codes on your device only. Spotlight indexing is off while App
  lock is on, and the Recent scans widget hides its content while App lock is on.

## 4. Third-party services

None built in. No third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software. Crash and performance
reports from Apple's MetricKit stay on your device; you can choose to attach them to a support e-mail. If you share
analytics with developers in iOS settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. Once a day the app asks the App
Store whether a newer version exists; the request contains only the app's ID and your store country.

## 5. Sharing, selling, children

We do not sell, rent or share any information. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including
children.

## 6. Keeping and deleting data

Everything stays on your devices, in your own device backups and, if you turned on iCloud sync, in your own iCloud
Drive. Delete entries in History (they wait 30 days in Recently Deleted, or empty it yourself), let ScanIt delete old
scans automatically (Settings › History › Keep for; favourites stay), delete codes in Your codes, or delete the app to
remove its data from the device. To remove synced copies, turn off iCloud sync and delete ScanIt's data in iOS
Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage. Backup files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

## 7. Honest limits

The link check looks only at a link's address with fixed rules (for example look-alike names, short links, bare
number addresses, unusual endings, app or profile downloads). It cannot see what a page contains and is not virus or
malware protection. "No warning signs" means none of those rules matched, not that a site is safe.
