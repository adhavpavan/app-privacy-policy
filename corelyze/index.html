Privacy Policy

Corelyze: CPU & Device Info for Android · Effective date: 17 October 2026

In short: Corelyze reads your phone's hardware and software details on the phone itself and shows them to you. There is no account and no server of ours, and your device information is never uploaded. To fix crashes and understand how the app is used in general, the app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) and Firebase Analytics (basic app events such as app opens and screens viewed, with device identifiers including the advertising ID). Pro is bought through Google Play. We never sell data.

2.Permissions

Corelyze needs no permission to show device information. These optional permissions are requested only when you tap the feature that needs them; you can refuse or revoke them at any time.

  • Notifications — only for the charge alarm and overheat alert you turn on.
  • Precise location — Android requires it to show your Wi-Fi network name and for the GPS test. Used only while the screen is open, never in the background, never stored or sent.
  • Phone — to show SIM details such as the data network type. The app never reads your phone number, IMEI or other identifiers.
  • Camera — camera test preview only. Nothing is recorded or saved. (Camera specifications need no permission.)
  • Microphone — microphone test: records 3 seconds, plays it back and deletes it.
  • Physical activity — step counter sensor test only.
  • Normal permissions declared without prompts: internet and network state (Firebase, Google Play), Wi-Fi state (Wi-Fi details), vibration and biometrics (hardware tests), run at startup (re-schedule widget refresh), advertising ID (Firebase Analytics).

3.Exports, sharing and feedback

When you copy, share or export information (text, PDF, JSON), the file is created on your phone and goes only where you send it. Reports contain device details, not personal data, unless you add your own notes. When you use "Contact us", your e-mail app opens with the app version, Android version and phone model added so we can help.

4.Third-party services

The app includes Google components that follow Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy):

  • Firebase Crashlytics (release builds) — crash and error reports: stack traces, device model, Android version, app version, free memory/disk at the time, a Crashlytics installation ID. https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Firebase Analytics (release builds) — automatically collected events (first open, sessions, screens viewed, in-app purchase events) with an app instance ID and the advertising ID. No device-info values are logged.
  • Google Play Billing — the one-time Pro purchase. Google handles payment; the app only learns whether your Google account owns Pro.
  • Google Play in-app updates and in-app review — to offer updates and, occasionally, a review prompt.
  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform — included but turned off: no ads are shown and AdMob is never started. If ads are ever turned on, this policy and an in-app consent screen will say so first.

5.Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. Google processes the Firebase and Play data above as our service provider.

6.Children

Corelyze is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7.Keeping and deleting data

Data stored by the app stays on your phone until you delete it; uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes all of it. Firebase keeps crash reports for about 90 days and analytics data according to its retention settings. To ask for deletion of crash or analytics data, write to tecdroiddeveloper@gmail.com. Deletion page: https://adhavpavan.github.io/app-privacy-policy/corelyze/delete-data/

8.Security

App data is kept in Android's private app storage. Connections to Google services are encrypted (HTTPS).

9.Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The current version, with its effective date, is always in the app (menu → Privacy policy) and at https://adhavpavan.github.io/app-privacy-policy/corelyze/.
