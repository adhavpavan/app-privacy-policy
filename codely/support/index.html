Support

Codely for iPhone and iPad

Need help? Write to tecdroiddeveloper@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days. Please include your device model, iOS version and what you were trying to do (Settings › Contact support fills in the app version for you).

1.Getting started

  • Scan: open the app (it starts on the camera) and point at a code. Tap the result to see what's inside before anything opens.
  • Photos, screenshots, PDFs: on Home tap Photo or PDF or file (or Paste for a copied image), or in another app tap Share › "Scan with Codely".
  • The camera doesn't open by itself: that's on purpose. Tap Scan on Home. To open the scanner at launch, turn on Settings › Scanner › Open the scanner when Codely opens.
  • Several codes in view: each gets a number. Tap the one you want, or tap Add all.
  • Backup: Settings › Data & backup › Back up to a file. Restore adds what's missing and never deletes.
  • Make a code: Create tab › pick a type › fill in › Save to My codes, Save to Photos or Share. Save is available once the code has been read back on your device.

2.Common questions

  • What does the link check do? It reads the link's address on your device and looks for warning signs: look-alike names of well-known sites, special characters that imitate letters, short links, bare number addresses, unusual endings, app or profile downloads, http without encryption. It cannot see the page itself and is not virus protection; Safari's own warnings still apply.
  • The camera is black or asks for permission: Settings app › Codely › Camera. Photo and PDF scanning work without it.
  • "Join network" fails: check the password in the code; iOS shows its own confirmation. Some networks (enterprise / captive portal) can't be joined from a code.
  • My made code says "Doesn't scan": use more contrast (dark code on light background), a quiet zone of 4, square dots or remove the logo.
  • Wi-Fi passwords in History: removed by default. Settings › Keep Wi-Fi passwords in History to keep them.
  • Export History: History › More › Export CSV.
  • Widget: Home Screen › Edit › Add Widget › Codely › My code. Choose which code in My codes (touch and hold › Show on widget).
  • Control Center: Control Center › + › Add a Control › Codely "Scan code".
  • UPI codes: Codely makes a static code with your own UPI ID. It is not affiliated with NPCI or any bank and never handles money; check the code with your UPI app before sharing it.
  • Look and language: Settings › Appearance; Settings › Language opens the Settings app.
  • App lock: Settings › App lock. Your device passcode always works as a fallback.
