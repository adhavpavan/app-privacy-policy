Privacy Policy

Codely for iPhone and iPad · Effective 2026-11-16 (updated 2026-11-16)

In short: Codely collects no data. No account, no servers of ours, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The codes you scan and make stay on your device.

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, calendar, location, photos or any identifier. The link check runs entirely on your device: links are never sent to us or to any "safe browsing" service.

2.Permissions (all optional)

  • Camera: to scan codes live. Without it you can still scan photos, screenshots and PDFs.
  • Add to Photos (add-only): when you tap "Save to Photos" for a code you made. The app cannot see your library.
  • Face ID / Touch ID (optional App lock): iOS checks it; the app only learns whether it succeeded.
  • Join Wi-Fi: when you tap "Join network", the network name and password go to iOS, which asks you to confirm.
  • Contacts and Calendar: not read. "Add to Contacts" and "Add to Calendar" open Apple's own screens; you decide whether to save.

3.When something leaves your device: only when you tap

  • Opening a link opens it in Safari. The website then receives what any visited website receives.
  • Product lookup ("Look up food", "Look up book", "Search the web") opens the public website of Open Food Facts, Open Library or DuckDuckGo with the barcode number in the address. The app itself makes no request.
  • Open in Maps / UPI app / Mail / Messages / Phone hands the code's content to that app.
  • Share and export (images, SVG, CSV, JSON, PDF, ZIP, backup files) go only where you send them.
  • Print sends the page to the printer you pick in the iOS print sheet.

4.Third-party services

None built in. No third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software. If you share analytics with developers in iOS settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. Once a day the app asks the App Store whether a newer version exists; the request contains only the app's ID and your store country.

5.Sharing, selling, children

We do not sell, rent or share any information. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

6.Keeping and deleting data

Everything stays on your device and in your own device backups. Delete single entries or everything in History (History › More › Delete all), let Codely delete old scans automatically (Settings › Data & backup › Keep history; favourites stay), delete codes in My codes, or delete the app to remove all its data. Backup files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

7.Honest limits

The link check looks only at a link's address with fixed rules (for example look-alike names, short links, bare number addresses, unusual endings, app or profile downloads). It cannot see what a page contains and is not virus or malware protection.
