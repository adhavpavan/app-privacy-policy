# Privacy Policy — Chiplens for iPhone and iPad

Effective date: 2026-11-24

**In short:** Chiplens collects no data. No account, no servers, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. What you create
stays on your device.

This policy explains how Chiplens ("the app", "we"), bundle ID `com.techdev.chiplens`, handles information.

## 1. What the app uses, and where it stays
| Information | Why | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| Device facts (model, chip, memory, storage, battery level, network type, sensors) | To show them to you | Read live on your device; never sent anywhere |
| Battery Analytics file you import | To show battery health and cycles | Parsed on your device; only the extracted numbers are kept, on your device |
| Battery level history, Checkup results, settings | History charts, results, your preferences | On your device (the app's private storage and its widgets) |
| Questions to "Ask about my device" | To answer them | Answered on your device by Apple Intelligence; not stored by us, not sent to us |
| Purchases (if offered) | To unlock Pro features | Handled by Apple. We receive no payment details |

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, location, photos or any identifier.

## 2. Permissions
- **Notifications** (optional): the charge alert you set up, created on your device.
- **Microphone and Camera** (optional): only during the matching Hardware Checkup test. Nothing is recorded, saved
  or sent.
- **Face ID / Touch ID** (optional App lock and the Checkup test): iOS checks it; the app only learns whether it
  succeeded.

## 3. Third-party services
None. No third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software. If you share analytics with
developers in iOS settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. Once a day the app asks the App Store whether a
newer version exists; the request contains only the app's ID and your store country.

## 4. Sharing, selling, children
We do not sell, rent or share any information. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including
children.

## 5. Keeping and deleting data
Everything stays on your device and in your own device backups. Delete the app to remove all its data.

## 6. Changes
We update this page when the app changes; the effective date is at the top.
