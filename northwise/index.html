Privacy Policy

Northwise: Digital Compass for Android · Effective date: 25 October 2026

In short: Northwise shows your heading, the sun and the moon, and keeps what you save on your phone. There is no account and no server of ours. Location is optional: it is asked only when you tap "Use my location", used only while the app is open, never in the background, and never sent anywhere. Camera pictures are only shown on screen to aim and are never saved or sent. To fix crashes and understand how the app is used in general, it uses Google Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) and Firebase Analytics (basic app events such as app opens and screens viewed, with device identifiers including the advertising ID). This version shows no ads and sells nothing. We never sell data.

2.Permissions

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) — optional. Asked only when you tap "Use my location", after a short explanation. It is used only while you use the app, never in the background: to set your position for true north and sun times, to show live coordinates, altitude and speed, and to keep "Here" current in Point to a place. You may allow only approximate location. Your location is never sent anywhere. If you refuse, the app works fully with a city or typed coordinates; you can turn the permission off at any time in Android settings.
  • Camera (CAMERA) — only for the camera sight in the Sight tab. Asked only when you turn the camera on. The picture is shown on screen to aim and is never saved or sent. If you refuse, the sighting tape works without it.
  • Normal permissions granted at install without a prompt: internet and network state (Firebase and Google Play) and advertising ID (Firebase Analytics). Libraries in the app may add related normal permissions (for example Android's ad-services permissions from the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the Play install referrer used by Firebase Analytics, or background-work permissions used by Android's WorkManager to refresh widgets); they are not used to collect anything beyond what this policy describes.
  • Northwise has no microphone permission, no background location and no access to your storage.

3.Sharing, backups and feedback

When you share a bearing as an image card, the image is created on your phone and sent only with the app you pick in the share sheet; it shows the bearing's name, bearing, back-bearing and north reference, not your position. Android may include the app's settings, saved position, bearings and places in your own Google account backup if you turned device backup on; that is handled by Android and Google, not by us. When you use "Contact us", your e-mail app opens with the app version, Android version and phone model added so we can help; you decide whether to send it.

4.Third-party services

The app includes Google components that follow Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy):

  • Firebase Crashlytics (release builds) — crash and error reports: stack traces, device model, Android version, app version, free memory and disk at the time, and a Crashlytics installation ID. https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Firebase Analytics (release builds) — automatically collected events (first open, sessions, screens viewed) with an app instance ID and the advertising ID. Your position, location, bearings, places and camera pictures are not sent.
  • Google Play in-app updates and in-app review — to offer updates and, occasionally, a review prompt.
  • Google Play Billing — included for a possible future one-time "Pro" purchase, but not active in this version: every feature is free and nothing can be bought.
  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform — included for possible future ads, but not active in this version: no ads are shown and AdMob is never started. If ads or purchases are ever turned on, this policy will be updated first and the app will ask for consent where the law requires it. Your location will never be passed to ads.

5.Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. Google processes the Firebase and Play data above as our service provider.

6.Children

Northwise is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7.Keeping and deleting data

Your saved position, bearings, places and settings stay on your phone until you delete them. You can delete bearings and places in the app and remove the saved position on the Position screen, or remove everything by uninstalling the app or via Android Settings › Apps › Northwise › Storage › Clear storage. Images you shared are not touched; delete them where you sent them. Firebase keeps crash reports for about 90 days and analytics data according to its retention settings. To ask for deletion of crash or analytics data, write to tecdroiddeveloper@gmail.com. Deletion page: https://adhavpavan.github.io/app-privacy-policy/northwise/delete-data/

8.Security

App data is kept in Android's private app storage. Connections to Google services are encrypted (HTTPS). No location, camera picture, bearing or place is sent anywhere by the app.

9.Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The current version, with its effective date, is always in the app (menu › Privacy policy) and at https://adhavpavan.github.io/app-privacy-policy/northwise/.
