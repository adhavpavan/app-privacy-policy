Privacy Policy

Measurely: Sound, Level, Lux for Android · Effective date: 19 October 2026

In short: Measurely measures sound, angle, light, motion and air pressure on your phone, and keeps what you record there. There is no account and no server of ours. Microphone sound is analysed in memory to compute the level and spectrum and is never recorded, saved or uploaded; camera frames are only shown on screen. The app does not use your location. To fix crashes and understand how the app is used in general, it uses Google Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) and Firebase Analytics (basic app events such as app opens and screens viewed, with device identifiers including the advertising ID). This version shows no ads and sells nothing. We never sell data.

2.Permissions

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) — for the sound meter, spectrum and noise recordings. Asked the first time you open a sound tool, with an explanation. The sound is analysed in memory only; no audio is ever stored or sent. You can refuse or turn it off in Android settings; the other tools keep working.
  • Camera (CAMERA) — only for the inclinometer's optional camera sight. Asked only when you turn the sight on. Frames are shown on screen and never saved. You can refuse; the plain sight keeps working.
  • Normal permissions granted at install without a prompt: internet and network state (Firebase and Google Play), vibration (haptic feedback), and advertising ID (Firebase Analytics). Libraries in the app may add related normal permissions (for example Android's ad-services permissions from the Google Mobile Ads SDK, or background-work permissions used by Android's WorkManager for widgets); they are not used to collect anything beyond what this policy describes.
  • Measurely has no location permission, no notifications in this version, and no access to your storage: exports use Android's file picker.

3.Exports, backups and feedback

When you export a session (CSV, JSON or a PDF noise report), the file is created on your phone and saved only where you choose with Android's file picker, or sent with the app you pick in the share sheet. A noise report contains the readings, the place and notes you typed, your phone model, Android version and the calibration used; it goes only where you send it. Android may include the app's settings and history in your own Google account backup if you turned device backup on; that is handled by Android and Google, not by us. When you use "Contact us", your e-mail app opens with the app version, Android version and phone model added so we can help; you decide whether to send it.

4.Third-party services

The app includes Google components that follow Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy):

  • Firebase Crashlytics (release builds) — crash and error reports: stack traces, device model, Android version, app version, free memory and disk at the time, and a Crashlytics installation ID. https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Firebase Analytics (release builds) — automatically collected events (first open, sessions, screens viewed) with an app instance ID and the advertising ID. Your readings, recordings, notes and audio are not sent.
  • Google Play in-app updates and in-app review — to offer updates and, occasionally, a review prompt.
  • Google Play Billing — included for a possible future one-time "Pro" purchase, but not active in this version: every feature is free and nothing can be bought.
  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform — included for possible future ads, but not active in this version: no ads are shown and AdMob is never started. If ads or purchases are ever turned on, this policy will be updated first and the app will ask for consent where the law requires it.

5.Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. Google processes the Firebase and Play data above as our service provider.

6.Children

Measurely is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7.Keeping and deleting data

Your recordings and calibrations stay on your phone until you delete them. You can delete sessions in the app (History, or Settings › Delete all sessions), or remove everything by uninstalling the app or via Android Settings › Apps › Measurely › Storage › Clear storage. Files you exported are not touched; delete them where you saved them. Firebase keeps crash reports for about 90 days and analytics data according to its retention settings. To ask for deletion of crash or analytics data, write to tecdroiddeveloper@gmail.com with the app instance ID shown in the app's About screen. Deletion page: https://adhavpavan.github.io/app-privacy-policy/measurely/delete-data/

8.Security

App data is kept in Android's private app storage. Connections to Google services are encrypted (HTTPS). No audio, camera image, reading or recording is sent anywhere by the app.

9.Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes, for example if a later version adds background noise logging with a notification. The current version, with its effective date, is always in the app (menu › Privacy policy) and at https://adhavpavan.github.io/app-privacy-policy/measurely/.
