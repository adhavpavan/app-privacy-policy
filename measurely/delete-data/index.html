Measurely: Sound, Level, Lux (com.techdev.measurely) · developer TechDev · Privacy policy

Data on your phone

Measurely has no accounts. Your settings, calibrations and recorded sessions are stored only on your phone. Microphone sound and camera frames are never stored at all. To delete your data, do either of these:

  • Android Settings › Apps › Measurely › Storage › Clear storage, or
  • uninstall Measurely. You can also delete sessions in the app (History, or Settings › Delete all sessions).

CSV, JSON and PDF files you exported yourself are not touched; delete them where you saved them.

Crash reports and analytics (Firebase)

The app sends crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and general usage analytics (Firebase Analytics), linked to a random app installation ID and the advertising ID, not to your name or e-mail. To have this data deleted:

  1. E-mail tecdroiddeveloper@gmail.com with the subject “Measurely data deletion request”. If you can, include the app instance ID shown in the app’s About screen.
  2. We reply within 30 days and delete the crash and analytics data we can match to your request in Firebase. Because the app has no accounts, we may ask for details such as the date and time you used the app or your phone model to find it.

Without a request, Firebase deletes this data automatically: crash reports after 90 days and user-level analytics data after 2 months. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

What is kept

Nothing. We keep no other copy of your data, and we never sell or share it.
