Privacy Policy

Chargewise: Battery Health for Android · Effective date: 19 October 2026

In short: Chargewise reads your phone's battery information on the phone itself and keeps your battery history there. There is no account and no server of ours, and your battery data is never uploaded. To fix crashes and understand how the app is used in general, the app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports) and Firebase Analytics (basic app events such as app opens and screens viewed, with device identifiers including the advertising ID). This version shows no ads and sells nothing. We never sell data.

1.What the app processes, and where

All processing happens on your device. What the app stores is kept only in its private storage (a local database and settings), which other apps cannot read.

Chargewise does not read your location, contacts, files, photos, messages, accounts or the list of your apps.

2.Permissions

Chargewise needs no permission to show battery information.

  • Notifications (Android 13 and newer) — only to ring the charge alarms and show the alerts you turn on. Asked the first time you turn one on. You can refuse or turn it off in Android settings; the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Normal permissions granted at install without a prompt: internet and network state (Firebase and Google Play), run at startup (to restart background battery checks after a reboot), vibration (alarm vibration), keep the processor awake briefly and background work (used by Android's WorkManager to run the background battery checks), and advertising ID (Firebase Analytics).

3.Exports, backups and feedback

When you export your history as a CSV file, the file is created on your phone and saved only where you choose with Android's file picker; it goes only where you send it. Android may include the app's settings and history in your own Google account backup if you turned device backup on; that is handled by Android and Google, not by us. When you use "Contact us", your e-mail app opens with the app version, Android version and phone model added so we can help; you decide whether to send it.

4.Third-party services

The app includes Google components that follow Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy):

  • Firebase Crashlytics (release builds) — crash and error reports: stack traces, device model, Android version, app version, free memory and disk at the time, and a Crashlytics installation ID. https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Firebase Analytics (release builds) — automatically collected events (first open, sessions, screens viewed) with an app instance ID and the advertising ID. Your battery readings and history are not sent.
  • Google Play in-app updates and in-app review — to offer updates and, occasionally, a review prompt.
  • Google Play Billing — included for a possible future one-time "Pro" purchase, but not active in this version: every feature is free and nothing can be bought.
  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform — included for possible future ads, but not active in this version: no ads are shown and AdMob is never started. If ads or purchases are ever turned on, this policy will be updated first and the app will ask for consent where the law requires it.

5.Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. Google processes the Firebase and Play data above as our service provider.

6.Children

Chargewise is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7.Keeping and deleting data

Your battery history stays on your phone until you delete it. You can clear it in the app (Settings), or remove everything by uninstalling the app or via Android Settings › Apps › Chargewise › Storage › Clear storage. Firebase keeps crash reports for about 90 days and analytics data according to its retention settings. To ask for deletion of crash or analytics data, write to tecdroiddeveloper@gmail.com with the app instance ID shown in the app's About screen. Deletion page: https://adhavpavan.github.io/app-privacy-policy/chargewise/delete-data/

8.Security

App data is kept in Android's private app storage. Connections to Google services are encrypted (HTTPS). No battery data is sent anywhere.

9.Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes, for example when a new feature reads new data. The current version, with its effective date, is always in the app (menu › Privacy policy) and at https://adhavpavan.github.io/app-privacy-policy/chargewise/.
