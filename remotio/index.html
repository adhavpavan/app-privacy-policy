Privacy Policy

Remotio for iPhone and iPad · Effective 2026-11-16

In short: Remotio collects no data. No account, no servers of ours, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app talks only to your TV and the smart-home devices you add on your home network and, if you allow it, to Apple Home (HomeKit) on your own devices.

2.Local network

To find your TV and send buttons to it, Remotio needs iOS Local Network access. With it, the app:

  • looks for TVs with Bonjour (the same discovery AirPlay uses) and by briefly checking addresses on your own Wi-Fi network for the ports smart TVs use;
  • reads basic information TVs share on the network (name, model, network address) to show them in a list;
  • sends remote commands and typed text to the TV you chose;
  • finds, pairs with and controls the Wi-Fi smart-home devices you add in the Home tab (Bonjour, or the IP address you type).

None of this traffic leaves your home network. You can turn Local Network off any time in Settings › Remotio; the demo TV still works.

3.Apple Home (HomeKit)

The Home tab shows accessories and scenes from Apple Home. The app asks for HomeKit access only when you open that tab and tap Continue. With it, the app reads your homes, rooms, accessories and scenes, changes accessories when you tap them (for example the AC temperature), runs scenes, and creates a "Movie mode" scene only after you confirm the list of what it does. HomeKit is Apple's framework: your home data is stored and synced by Apple Home on your devices (and iCloud, if you use it). Remotio has no server; nothing about your home is sent to us or to anyone else. iPhone has no infrared. Without Apple Home access, the TV remote and the Wi-Fi devices you add still work, and you can try a demo home with made-up accessories. You can change the permission any time in Settings › Remotio.

4.Other permissions

  • Face ID / Touch ID (optional App lock): iOS checks it; the app only learns whether it succeeded.
  • Live Activities (optional Lock Screen remote): shown only when you turn it on from the remote screen.

The app does not ask for notifications, microphone, camera, photos, location or contacts.

5.Third-party services

None. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and no ad SDK. If you share analytics with developers in iOS settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. Once a day the app asks Apple's App Store whether a newer version exists; that request contains only the app's ID and your store country. TV brand names are trademarks of their owners; Remotio is not affiliated with any TV maker.

6.Sharing, selling, children

We do not sell, rent or share any information. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

7.Keeping and deleting data

Everything stays on your device and in your own device backups (Keychain items are device-only). To remove it: TVs › swipe a TV › Forget (removes the TV and its pairing keys), Settings › Forget all pairings, or delete the app. Your Apple Home data is managed in Apple's Home app; deleting Remotio does not change it (a Movie mode scene you created stays in the Home app until you delete it there).
