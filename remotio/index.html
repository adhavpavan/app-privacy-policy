Privacy Policy

Remote-IO for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch · Effective 2026-11-21

In short: Remote-IO collects no data. No account, no servers of ours, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app talks only to your TV and the smart-home devices you add on your home network and, if you allow it, to Apple Home (HomeKit) on your own devices. If you keep iCloud sync on, your TV list and pairings are kept in your own iCloud by Apple, where we can't see them.

2.Local network

To find your TV and send buttons to it, Remote-IO needs iOS Local Network access. With it, the app:

  • looks for TVs with Bonjour (the same discovery AirPlay uses) and by briefly checking addresses on your own Wi-Fi network for the ports smart TVs use;
  • reads basic information TVs share on the network (name, model, network address) to show them in a list;
  • sends remote commands and typed text to the TV you chose;
  • finds, pairs with and controls the Wi-Fi smart-home devices you add in the Home tab (Bonjour, or the IP address you type).

None of this traffic leaves your home network. You can turn Local Network off any time in Settings › Remote-IO; the demo TV still works.

3.Apple Home (HomeKit)

The Home tab shows accessories and scenes from Apple Home. The app asks for HomeKit access only when you open that tab and tap Continue. With it, the app reads your homes, rooms, accessories and scenes, changes accessories when you tap them (for example the AC temperature), runs scenes, and creates a "Movie mode" scene only after you confirm the list of what it does. HomeKit is Apple's framework: your home data is stored and synced by Apple Home on your devices (and iCloud, if you use it). Remote-IO has no server; nothing about your home is sent to us or to anyone else. iPhone has no infrared. Without Apple Home access, the TV remote and the Wi-Fi devices you add still work, and you can try a demo home with made-up accessories. You can change the permission any time in Settings › Remote-IO.

4.iCloud sync

Settings › iCloud › Sync TVs and pairings with iCloud (on by default) keeps your TV list (names, brand/platform, model, network address, MAC address if known), your room scenes (name, icon and steps, which name a TV or Home device by its id) and a few settings (appearance, accent, tabs, haptics, remote options) in your own iCloud key-value storage, and the TV pairing keys in your iCloud Keychain, which Apple encrypts end to end. Your Apple Account is the only account: Remote-IO has no server, and the developer has no access to any of it. Pairing keys of Wi-Fi Home devices (Hue, Nanoleaf, Home Assistant) sync only if you also turn on Also sync Home device keys (off by default). Turn sync off any time: this device keeps its own copy and stops syncing, and your other devices keep the copies already in your iCloud. To remove pairing keys from iCloud Keychain too, use Settings › Forget all pairings while sync is on. If you're not signed in to iCloud, nothing syncs and the app works the same.

5.Other permissions

  • Face ID / Touch ID (optional App lock): iOS checks it; the app only learns whether it succeeded.
  • Live Activities (optional Lock Screen remote): shown only when you turn it on from the remote screen.
  • Apple Watch (optional): the watch app asks for no permissions. It gets your TV names, the current TV and your accent colour from Remote-IO on your iPhone through Apple's WatchConnectivity (directly between your devices, no server of ours), and each button you press goes back the same way: the iPhone sends it to the TV over your Wi-Fi. The watch never talks to the TV itself.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition (optional voice search): asked only when you first tap the microphone. Your speech is turned into text on your device by Apple's on-device speech recognition; the text goes only to your TV as a search. No audio is stored or sent to us or anyone else.
  • Share to Remote-IO (optional share extension): when you share a link to Remote-IO from another app, it sends that link only to your TV on your home network so the TV can open it. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere else.

The app does not ask for notifications, camera, photos, location or contacts.

6.Third-party services

None. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and no ad SDK. If you share analytics with developers in iOS settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. Once a day the app asks Apple's App Store whether a newer version exists; that request contains only the app's ID and your store country. TV brand names are trademarks of their owners; Remote-IO is not affiliated with any TV maker.

7.Sharing, selling, children

We do not sell, rent or share any information. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

8.Keeping and deleting data

Everything stays on your device, in your own device backups and, with iCloud sync on, in your own iCloud. To remove it: TVs › swipe a TV › Forget (removes the TV and its pairing keys, also from your other devices when sync is on), Settings › Forget all pairings, Settings › iCloud (turn sync off), or delete the app. Your Apple Home data is managed in Apple's Home app; deleting Remote-IO does not change it (a Movie mode scene you created stays in the Home app until you delete it there).
