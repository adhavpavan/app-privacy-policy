Support

Remote-IO for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch

Need help? Write to tecdroiddeveloper@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days. Please include your device model, iOS version and what you were trying to do (Settings › Contact support fills in the app version for you).

1.Getting started

  1. Turn on your TV and connect this iPhone or iPad to the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Open Remote-IO, tap Continue and allow Local Network when iOS asks.
  3. In TVs, choose your TV. Samsung and LG ask you to Allow on the TV; Google TV / Android TV and Fire TV show a code to type.

No TV nearby? Tap Try the demo TV.

2.Your TV

  • Roku: if buttons don't work, set Settings › System › Advanced system settings › Control by mobile apps › Network access to Enabled (older software: Permissive).
  • Samsung (2016+): choose Allow on the TV. If you chose Deny: Settings › General › External Device Manager › Device Connection Manager.
  • LG webOS: choose Accept on the TV within about 30 seconds.
  • Google TV / Android TV (Sony, TCL, Philips, Hisense, Xiaomi, Chromecast with Google TV…): type the 6-character code shown on the TV.
  • Fire TV (beta): wake it with its own remote, then type the 4-digit code.

Menu names differ between models and software versions. Brand names are trademarks of their owners; Remote-IO is not affiliated with any TV maker.

3.Common questions

  • Remote-IO can't find my TV: make sure the TV is on, you're on the same Wi-Fi (not a guest network) and VPN is off. Then TVs › Scan again, or Add by IP address.
  • Buttons stopped working: your TV may have a new address; scan again in TVs. Check Settings › Remote-IO › Local Network is on.
  • Does it use infrared? No. iPhone and iPad have no infrared. Remote-IO controls smart TVs over Wi-Fi, Wi-Fi lights and plugs it supports directly, and accessories in Apple Home or your own Home Assistant.
  • Air conditioners and lights (Home tab): Remote-IO shows what's in Apple Home: HomeKit or Matter ACs, thermostats, lights, plugs, fans, air purifiers, blinds, locks and sensors, plus your scenes. Wi-Fi lights and plugs work directly (Philips Hue with the bridge, Shelly, Nanoleaf, WLED, Elgato, WiZ, Yeelight, Govee with LAN Control, LIFX), and so does your own Home Assistant. An AC with only an infrared remote works when an IR hub adds it to Apple Home (for example SwitchBot Hub 2 or another Matter IR bridge). Allow Apple Home when asked; to change it later, open the iOS Settings app › Remote-IO. No accessories yet? Try the demo home in the Home tab.
  • An accessory says "Not responding": check it has power and is in range of your home hub (Apple TV or HomePod), then pull down to refresh.
  • Turning the TV on: works when the TV keeps its network on in standby (Roku TV: Fast TV start; Samsung: Power On with Mobile; LG: Turn on via Wi-Fi).
  • Apple Watch: the watch remote sends every button through Remote-IO on your iPhone (watchOS can't talk to TVs on your Wi-Fi itself), so keep the iPhone nearby with Bluetooth or Wi-Fi on; it doesn't need to be unlocked or have the app open. Add and pair TVs on the iPhone first; they appear on the watch by the same names. Tap the TV icon to switch TVs, turn the Digital Crown for volume, and swipe for the second page (volume, power). "iPhone not reachable" means the watch can't see your iPhone; "isn't answering" means the TV didn't respond (on? same Wi-Fi as the iPhone?). Add the Remote-IO complication to a watch face or the Smart Stack to open it in one tap.
  • Using an iPad or a second iPhone? With Settings › iCloud › Sync TVs and pairings with iCloud on (the default) and the same Apple Account, your TVs and their pairings appear on your other devices by themselves, so you don't pair again. Everything is kept in your own iCloud and iCloud Keychain; we can't see it. Home device keys (Hue, Nanoleaf, Home Assistant) sync only if you turn on Also sync Home device keys. Not signed in to iCloud? Everything still works on each device.
  • Voice search: tap the microphone and speak; your words are recognised on your device and sent only to your TV as a search. iOS asks for Microphone and Speech Recognition the first time.
  • Send a link to your TV: in another app tap Share › Remote-IO. The link goes only to your TV on your home network.
  • Apple Watch tips: hold a D-pad or volume button to repeat it; double tap (pinch twice) to play or pause.
  • Widgets and Control Center: Settings › Widgets & controls explains how to add them.
  • Is it free? Yes. Every button is free, no ads, no account.
